The rules

  • EMA crossover: on 5-minute Nifty candles, buy a call (CE) when the 9-candle average crosses above the 21-candle average, a put (PE) when it crosses below. Exit when they cross back.
  • Opening range breakout: take Nifty's high and low from 9:15 to 9:30. Buy CE when a candle closes above the high, PE when one closes below the low. Exit if Nifty falls back past the middle of that range.
  • RSI reversal: buy CE when RSI(14) climbs back above 30 (oversold), PE when it drops back below 70 (overbought). Exit when RSI reaches 50.
Filters (the "+ filters" versions enter only if all six pass)
  • Expiry: uses the nearest weekly option with at least 2 days left, so fast time decay doesn't eat it.
  • Bigger trend: CE only if Nifty is above its 50-candle average (about 4 hours); PE only if below.
  • Open interest: looks at the 7 strikes around the current price over the last 15 minutes. CE only if put open interest grew more than call open interest (option sellers betting Nifty won't fall); PE the opposite.
  • India VIX: skip if VIX is below 11. Nifty barely moves then, and option buyers usually lose to time decay.
  • Not too expensive: the app works out the option's implied volatility (how much "fear" is priced in). Skip if it is more than 1.3 times India VIX.
  • Liquidity: skip if the gap between the best buy and sell price is more than ₹0.50 or 1% of the price.
For every trade
  • 1 lot of the at-the-money option. Plain versions use the nearest weekly expiry (next one on expiry day). New entries only 9:30 am–2:30 pm. Stop-loss −25% of premium, target +40%, everything closed by 3:15 pm. At most one trade per strategy per day. Skipped if 1 lot costs more than ₹20,000.
  • Daily pick: once any version has 10+ paper trades, the pick is the version with the best paper profit after charges (among those with 10+). Before that, the filtered version of the strategy with the best recent backtest. If nothing is in profit, the day is skipped.
  • Charges are estimated: ₹20 brokerage per order, 0.15% STT on the sell side, exchange fees, SEBI fee, stamp duty and 18% GST.
  • Keep this page open on one device from before 9:15 am to 3:30 pm. Zerodha requires a fresh login every morning.
