How it works, in simple words

Words you'll see

  • UP bet (Call): makes money if Nifty goes up.
  • DOWN bet (Put): makes money if Nifty goes down.
  • Single option: buy one option. Bigger wins and bigger losses.
  • Safer pair: buy one option and sell a cheaper one at the same time. Costs about a third, loses value more slowly, but the profit has a cap.
  • Loss limit: if the trade falls to this price, the app gets out to stop the loss growing.
  • Target: if the trade rises to this price, the app takes the profit.
  • Big players (OI): open interest shows how many option contracts are open. Big players usually sell options. When they sell lots of puts, they're betting Nifty won't fall; lots of calls, that it won't rise.
  • Floor and ceiling: the price levels where big players sold the most puts (floor) and calls (ceiling). Nifty often stops near them.
  • Fear index (VIX): how much traders expect Nifty to move. Very low means prices barely move, which is bad for option buyers.

The 3 ideas

  • Big players (OI): every 5 minutes it compares with 30 minutes ago. UP bet when futures traders are buying and more puts than calls were sold. DOWN bet in the opposite case.
  • Morning breakout: notes Nifty's high and low from 9:15 to 9:30. UP bet when Nifty breaks above that high; DOWN bet when it breaks below the low.
  • Trend turn: UP bet when Nifty's short-term average (last 45 min) crosses above its longer average (last 1¾ hours); DOWN bet when it crosses below.

Safety checks before every trade (all must pass)

  • Enough days left: uses an option with at least 2 days before expiry, so it doesn't lose value too fast.
  • Market direction agrees: UP bets only when Nifty is above its 4-hour average; DOWN bets only when below.
  • Big players agree: for the breakout and trend ideas, OI must point the same way.
  • Enough room to move: the ceiling (for UP) or floor (for DOWN) must be at least 75 points away.
  • Market is moving enough: fear index at 11 or above.
  • Price not too costly: skips options priced unusually high compared with the fear index.
  • Easy to buy and sell: the gap between buy and sell price must be small.

Trade rules

  • New trades only 9:30 am to 1:30 pm. Everything is closed by 3:15 pm. One trade per plan per day. Never more than ₹20,000 per trade.
  • Single option: loss limit −30%, target +50%. Once up 25%, the loss limit moves to +5% so a winner can't turn into a loser.
  • Safer pair: loss limit −35%, target +40%. Once up 20%, the loss limit moves to break-even.
  • Practice prices use the real buy and sell prices, and the results include all charges (brokerage, STT, exchange fees, GST, stamp duty).
  • Today's plan: the plan with the best profit after 10+ practice trades. Until one exists, the app tests plans one by one, safer pairs first.

Your daily routine

  • Before 9:15 am: open this page, log in to Zerodha, press Start. Keep the page open and the laptop awake until 3:30 pm.
  • Telegram tells you when the app starts, when today's plan buys and sells, and sends a summary after 3:30 pm.
  • Don't use real money until "Is it working?" turns green.
